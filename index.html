Privacy Policy

NearDoor uses account, selected location, post, request, message, notification and safety report information to run nearby discovery. We do not sell personal information.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as phone number, Google login details, display name and profile details.
  • Selected location details such as latitude, longitude, nearby area label and saved places.
  • User content such as posts, asks, optional images, messages, reports and seller verification submissions.
  • Notification preferences, device push tokens and delivery status.

How we use information

  • To show posts and requests inside the distance a user chooses.
  • To let users post, ask, reply, reserve, chat, report, block and manage their account.
  • To send phone OTP login messages and push notifications based on user preferences.
  • To moderate posts, review seller checks, investigate reports and reduce scams.

Location and photos

Users can use GPS or manually move a map pin. NearDoor stores the selected point so nearby posts work. Users may upload optional images for posts, asks and seller checks. Moderators may review content for safety.

Service providers

NearDoor uses providers such as Supabase for backend services, Firebase for push notifications, Twilio for phone OTP, and map/location providers for map display and geocoding.

Terms of Use

NearDoor connects nearby people. Users are responsible for what they post, request, agree to and do when they meet.

Rules

  • Use NearDoor lawfully, honestly and safely.
  • Do not post scams, fake pricing, fake location, harassment or illegal goods/services.
  • Only upload content you own or have permission to use.
  • NearDoor may approve, reject, close, remove or limit content/accounts for safety.

Payments

NearDoor does not currently process payments inside the app. Payment, delivery, pickup and service arrangements are between users.

Safety Center

NearDoor is for nearby discovery, but users should still verify, meet carefully and report anything suspicious.

  • Meet in a public place when possible.
  • Tell someone where you are going.
  • Do not share your exact home address too early.
  • Check the item, food, rental or service before paying.
  • Report scams, harassment, fake locations or unsafe behavior inside the app.

Support

The fastest way to report a post, request, user or app issue is from inside NearDoor. Open the post, request, chat or profile, then tap report or block when available.

If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. NearDoor reports help moderation, but they are not emergency support.

Delete Account

NearDoor includes an in-app account deletion flow. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

  1. Open NearDoor.
  2. Go to Me.
  3. Open Settings & safety.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm deletion.

Account deletion is designed to remove your profile, posts, requests, device push tokens and account activity from NearDoor systems.
